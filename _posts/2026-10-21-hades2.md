Death to Chronos! This week we brew up an incantation to summon the sequel of one of our top 5 roguelites of the podcast: Hades 2! The famed house of the Underworld has been captured by the Titan of Time and it's up to you: Melinoë, the sister of Zagreus hidden away by the Halloween hat touting goddess of the Crossroads Hecate. The only way to restore your family is to destroy time itself by delving down into the Underworld, but also by summiting Mount Olympus itself. It's twice the adventure, twice the polycule relationships, and twice the metaprogression resources! So mote it be!
